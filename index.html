What you can do

📋

Browse the menu

Categories, daily specials, search, sorting and favourites. Sold-out dishes are clearly marked.

🛒

Order your way

Dine-in at your table or takeaway at a pickup time, with notes like "no onions".

🧾

No bill surprises

Subtotal, service charge, tax and promo discount, all shown before you confirm.

⏱️

Track your order

Pending, Preparing, Ready, Served, with a live timer.

📅

Book a table

Pick a date, time and party size. Full slots are greyed out instantly.

👨‍🍳

Manager dashboard

Move orders through the kitchen, accept bookings and edit prices or availability.

Install

  1. Tap Download for Android on your phone.
  2. Open the downloaded RestaurantApp.apk.
  3. If Android asks, allow Install unknown apps for your browser.
  4. Tap Install, then open Restaurant App.
